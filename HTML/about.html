Our Story

Riding Nepal
Since 2015

Nepal MotorBike Rental Service was founded in Kathmandu in 2015 with a single dirtbike and a dream — to show the world the raw, unfiltered beauty of Nepal from the seat of a motorcycle.

What started as a passion project among a small group of riders quickly grew into Nepal's most trusted motorbike hire and adventure touring company. Today, we operate a fleet of 25+ conditioned machines and have led over 100 guided expeditions across the Himalayas, Terai, and everything in between.

We don't just rent bikes. We craft experiences. Every route is hand-scouted, every bike is personally inspected, and every customer is treated like a fellow rider — because to us, that's exactly what they are.

Our Core Values

01

Safety First

Every ride starts with a safety brief, every bike with a full inspection. We never cut corners when lives are on the line.

02

Passion for Riding

We are riders ourselves. That obsession lives in everything we do — from choosing routes to maintaining our fleet.
