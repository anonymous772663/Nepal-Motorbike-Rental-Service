Hire Policy

Rates & Rules

Before you book, please read our hire policies. We keep things simple and fair.

  • Valid driving license (Nepal or IDP) required
  • Minimum age: 18 years
  • Security deposit: NPR 10,000–20,000 (refundable)
  • Fuel at your own expense.
  • Damage assessed on return — fair market rate charged
  • 24-hour cancellation for full refund
  • Late return: Amount will be changed according to the delay
  • Off-road riding permitted — rider assumes trail risk
